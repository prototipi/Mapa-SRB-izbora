# Decisions and sources

This file records the data sources, matching rules and analysis choices behind the map pages (`mapa_izbora_sr-latn.html`, `mapa_izbora_sr-cyrl.html`, `mapa_izbora.html`).
The pages are built by `build_map.py`. Last updated: 29.10.2026.

## Source note from the page

This text was the footer of the side panel until 29.10.2026:

> Results: 2023: scraped from the RIK results page `rezultati_2023.xlsx`, data as of 9.01.2024 20:21. 2026: RIK polling-station export `14_30_09_29.10.2026.xlsx`, data as of 29.10.2026 14:25. Shares are of valid votes; turnout is of registered voters at reported stations. Belgrade shows its 17 city municipalities. Врање includes Врањска Бања. Пожаревац includes Костолац. Ниш includes Медијана, Нишка Бања, Палилула, Пантелеј, Црвени крст. Ужице includes Севојно. Boundaries: GADM 4.1 (Serbia), Natural Earth (other countries). Polling stations abroad are placed at their city (OpenStreetMap); 2023 cities from the MFA list of 14.12.2023, 2026 cities and addresses from the RIK decision.
